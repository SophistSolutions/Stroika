## Description

Possibly lose Optional<> altogether. After C++ 17 - the case is less strong for the class.
But there are still several advantages, so maybe keep.
But at any rate - make it easy to convert both ways. From optional<> is easy. To optional maybe use As<> or something similar. Maybe .optional or .asoptional? - something easy to type/use. Whatever I do here - consider doing likewise for String (.aswstring) or .wstring).
