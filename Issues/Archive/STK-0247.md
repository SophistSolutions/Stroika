## Description

NEW thoughts as of 2008-12-05
Consider losing ‘Foundation’ idea, and just having more layers. 
For example, 
“Process” – and put threading etc stuff under there.
HTML, and put whole suit of HTML/DOM handling / generation stuff there (like asp.net)
One advantage of non-layered approach is its easier to fit stuff in like Led. At the edges – the layering didn’t quite fit. It often did. But I’m not sure how much value it added. Maybe – in terms of separation and chunking? But that can be done with documentation artificats – as well, and there can be different conceptual views.
Another trick is to find a way to have smaller .obj granularity – so that dependencies are less tight. Not quite sure how to do this
