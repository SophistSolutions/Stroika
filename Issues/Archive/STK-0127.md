### Lewis Pringle - 2016-09-18T10:27:58.799-0400

issue was forward declare of iostream stuff:
Yay for psychic debugging powers!  (I was just reducing your smaller repro, so this saves me a bunch of time.)
If you need to forward-declare basic_istream and basic_ostream, you need to wrap them (and only them) in the following pragmas:
#pragma vtordisp(push, 2)   // compiler bug workaround
[...]
#pragma vtordisp(pop) // compiler bug workaround
These appear around their definitions, and affect layout.
