SSDPClient shows how to use the Stroika UPnP framework to find UPnP devices - searching for them (SSDP), or listening for
their announcements - and read each one's description, with its services. And to use a service: with --switch on (or off), it
switches each UPnP light it finds (each SwitchPower service, such as the SSDPServer sample's), with a SOAP request.
